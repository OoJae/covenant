Every wallet the team controls is listed here. The on-chain `TeamRegistry` lists them as they join it: the deployer is entry 0 of the registry from the block that creates the processor, any other wallet once a listed wallet has invited it and it has declared itself. Since 2026-10-06 (23:33 UTC) the registry lists all three: entry 0 the deployer, entry 1 the keeper (declared "keeper" in tx 0x88c4624b…fff1, its first transaction, before its first settle), entry 2 the Architect wallet (declared "architect (OKX.AI agent 14683)" in tx 0xb3038c17…c624, after the deployer's invite). The Architect wallet acted once before it was in the registry (its OKX.AI registration, a user operation that `tools/audit-team` lists). The deployer sent the Architect wallet 0.0002 OKB for that declaration's gas. The declaration then went through as a user operation (EntryPoint v0.7, in a bundler's transaction at gas price 0), so the wallet still held the 0.0002 OKB on 2026-10-08.
None of them ever buys, sells or swaps any IGNIX token, sends funds into a kernel, or trades transistors.
