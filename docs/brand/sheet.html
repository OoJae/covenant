§08Rules

Do and don’t

Only real artefacts: the netlist die, real state words, computed numbers. Every sentence checked against the claims audit.

Do

  1. 01Draw the Seal from a state word the chip actually holds, and say which.
  2. 02Keep gold for one thing per view: the action, pin 1, the active wire.
  3. 03Write plain verbs from the reader’s side: “Check it yourself”, “Run the eight checks”.
  4. 04Keep “Unaudited” and “Adoption is zero” wherever the claims are made.
  5. 05Put the word next to every verdict colour: MATCH, DIFFERENT, STALE.

Don’t

  1. 01Fill a grid with random bits for texture. If it looks like state, it must be state.
  2. 02Round a corner, add a shadow or a gradient that means nothing.
  3. 03Set Bodoni below 1.75rem, or the wordmark in capitals.
  4. 04Use a route colour for text, or for anything that is not money moving.
  5. 05Use stock photos, AI images or superlatives.
